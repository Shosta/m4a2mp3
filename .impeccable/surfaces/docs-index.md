## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page treats audio conversion as careful album publishing, where file metadata and cover art stay attached rather than being lost in format changes; it refuses the generic software hero and feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: A cover-layout composition built from deep blue-black ink, warm paper, and a vivid vermilion sleeve; large editorial lettering, printed rules, and compact colophon-like utility labels establish a music-release visual language.

STORY: A listener arrives with a mixed audio collection, sees the supported formats and preserved metadata, understands the local FFmpeg requirement, then chooses the Linux AppImage or source instructions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact masthead and language selector sit above an editorial composition: the headline and one-sentence offer occupy the left half, the illustrated temporary album sleeve/interface preview occupies the right, and the Linux AppImage action is a large high-contrast printed label beneath the offer. Source and license links remain secondary but visible.
